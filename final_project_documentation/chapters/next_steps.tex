Table~\ref{tab:troubleshooting} to connect common symptoms with diagnostic
commands and likely causes. We based these checks on the operational commands in
\texttt{Terraform-\allowbreak code/\allowbreak DEPLOYMENT.md} and the deployment mechanics in
Chapter~\ref{chap:Methodology}.
